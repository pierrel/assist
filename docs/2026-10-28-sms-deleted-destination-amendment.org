#+title: SMS receipt ACK after destination deletion
#+date: 2026-10-28

* Correction

The archive outlives a thread and its Run journal. If a phone loses its ACK and
retries after the destination is permanently deleted, the frozen route must not
recreate work or retry forever. Keep the archived receipt and routing decision;
acknowledge it with no Run when the thread entry is positively absent while its
parent store is accessible. This is the normal deletion boundary, not SMS
execution recovery. No tombstone or admission-progress mirror is added.

Use the entry itself as the signal, not a missing runs.json file. A broken
symlink, a non-directory entry, permission/EIO errors, or unavailable parent
storage is not evidence of deletion and remains unavailable. An extant directory
with no Run journal keeps normal RunService first-admission semantics. Frozen
routing is never replaced with a newly matching subscription.

* Verification

Synthetic real archive/Run state reproduces lost ACK, deletion of the complete
destination directory, and retry. The receipt remains unchanged, no Run or
scheduler action is recreated, and the phone receives its duplicate ACK.
Controls exercise unavailable parent storage, child permissions/EIO, a broken
symlink and a non-directory entry. Existing before-Run crash/retry and timestamp
cutoff tests still exercise ordinary first admission with an extant destination.
No model, SMS, phone or shared-state effects are needed.

* Historical supersessions

This narrows the ACK rule in /What is removed/ and /Verification and rollout/ of
[[file:2026-10-28-sms-ingestion-run-boundary.org][2026-10-28-sms-ingestion-run-boundary.org]]: after an explicit destination
deletion, a durable archived receipt does not require a new or surviving Run
before ACK. All other routing, trust and outcome boundaries remain unchanged.
